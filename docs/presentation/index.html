The distinction

Evidence first, not another reminder bot.

The differentiator is a source-first packet that separates exact excerpts from generated suggestions and keeps a person in control.

  • Only verbatim quotes that exist in stored sources are retained.
  • Model-generated narrative is discarded; gap suggestions are labeled for verification.
  • PayPal actions are separate from AI and require explicit user confirmation.

Novelty is conditional; existing invoice collection and automation products are acknowledged in the research.
